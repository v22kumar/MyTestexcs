\section{STAR --- the structure (rehearse now, facts from INTAKE.md)}
%=====================================================================
Every behavioural answer uses \textbf{STAR}: \textbf{S}ituation (brief context),
\textbf{T}ask (your responsibility), \textbf{A}ction (what \emph{you} did),
\textbf{R}esult (the measurable outcome). Keep it to about 2 minutes, own the
Action in the first person, and always land a concrete Result.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Difficult bug / technical challenge:}
  \textbf{[NEEDS INTAKE: B1 --- a real bug or challenge, with the measurable
  result. Frame it with Chapters 4/11 once supplied.]}
\item \textbf{Conflict / disagreement:}
  \textbf{[NEEDS INTAKE: B2 --- a real instance and how it was professionally
  resolved.]}
\item \textbf{Deadline pressure / prioritisation:}
  \textbf{[NEEDS INTAKE: B3 --- a real deadline, what you protected (e.g.\ test
  coverage/safety) and what you traded.]}
\item \textbf{Failure / what you'd do differently:}
  \textbf{[NEEDS INTAKE: B4 --- a real example showing learning, no blame.]}
\item \textbf{Recent learning / growth:}
  \textbf{[NEEDS INTAKE: B5 --- a real recent skill/tool/topic.]}
\end{itemize}
